评估造血干细胞移植住院患者饮食与肠道菌群动态的关系，并用小鼠实验考察蔗糖对抗生素诱导菌群变化的影响。

Among core-eligible reviews reporting standardised mean differences, exercise was associated with small reductions in tumour necrosis factor-alpha (TNF-alpha; point estimates - 1.42 to - 0.05, two of five confidence intervals excluding the null) and C-reactive protein (CRP; - 0.65 to - 0.47, two of three excluding the null), whereas interleukin-6 (IL-6) estimates were inconsistent (- 0.81 to + 0.09, two of five excluding the null).
finding_3
AMSTAR-2 confidence was critically low in 11 reviews, low in nine, and moderate in one; GRADE certainty was very low for every biomarker construct.
finding_4
Effects on immunosenescence and cellular-senescence markers remain an evidence gap. This biomarker evidence alone is insufficient to justify prescribing exercise specifically to lower inflammatory biomarkers and does not alter broader physical-activity recommendations based on clinical and functional outcomes.
